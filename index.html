What Are Aeroplanes?

An aeroplane is a powered aircraft with fixed wings. It uses engines to move forward and generate lift, allowing it to fly through the air.

Did you know? Aeroplanes can travel between countries in just a few hours!

Types of Aeroplanes

Here are three popular types of aircraft.

Passenger Planes

Carry passengers between cities and countries.

Private Planes

Small aircraft used for personal travel and training.

Cargo Planes

Transport packages, goods, and supplies around the world.

Why Are Aeroplanes Important?

  • They make long-distance travel faster.
  • They help connect people around the world.
  • They transport goods and emergency supplies.
  • They support tourism and international business.
